These are guidelines for maps and mods to be uploaded to the vault. Content that violates these may be hidden, however, they are not rules, and violations against them alone will not incur sanctions upon the uploader.

## General
* Names and descriptions should not be misleading, and should reasonably describe the content.
* Test content should only be uploaded to the vault if deemed necessary, should be removed once the 'test' is complete, and should not remain for an extended period of time.
    * A guide on testing maps outside of the vault [can be found here](https://youtu.be/EQbP_mXER7M).
* Content with excessively large file sizes had better be worth it.

## Maps
* Maps of size 2.56km and especially 1.28km are generally considered too small to be competitive, and thus should not be uploaded as ranked.
* Maps that use the output of a generator should include the seed, and have enough effort put into cleaning and decorating them such that they cannot be readily mistaken for the raw output of said generator.
* Map names and descriptions should avoid allusions and inclusions of real world place names unless such places are reasonably accurately depicted in the map.

## Mods
* Mods should avoid copying unmodified binary files from the base game instead of referencing them.
